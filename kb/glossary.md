# Glossary

## Capital terms
CET1 (common equity tier 1) capital is the highest-quality regulatory capital. The CET1 ratio divides it by
risk-weighted assets (RWA). The stress capital buffer (SCB) is the decline from the starting to the minimum
projected CET1 ratio under the severely adverse scenario plus four quarters of planned dividends, floored at 2.5
percent. PPNR is pre-provision net revenue, revenues minus non-interest expenses before credit provisions.

## Stress testing terms
CCAR and DFAST are the Federal Reserve's capital planning review and stress tests. The global market shock is an
instantaneous repricing of trading positions applied in the first quarter of the severely adverse scenario.

## Statistical terms
A chi-square plausibility p-value is the probability, under a model, of shocks at least as large as those
needed to produce a scenario. Conformal prediction builds intervals from past forecast errors with coverage that
does not rely on a distributional assumption; adaptive conformal inference updates its level after each miss.
The stationary block bootstrap resamples historical residuals in blocks of random length to keep crisis
clustering. Huber M-estimation down-weights outliers in regressions. The Lintner model describes dividends as
partially adjusting towards a target share of earnings.

# Federal Reserve 2026 severely adverse scenario: public reference points

## Headline variables
The Federal Reserve's 2026 severely adverse scenario describes a severe global recession triggered by an abrupt
decline in risk appetite. Published reference points: the unemployment rate rises 5.5 percentage points to a
peak of 10 percent; equity prices fall about 58 percent over the first three quarters; house prices fall about
30 percent and commercial real estate prices about 39 percent. Press coverage reports a VIX peak of about 72.
The proposed version of the scenario featured a BBB corporate spread widening of about 4.4 percentage points.
Sources: federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-stress-test-scenarios.htm; bpi.com coverage of the proposed 2026
scenarios.

## Results of the 2026 stress test
Under the severely adverse scenario, the aggregate common equity tier 1 capital ratio of the 32 participating
banks fell from 12.8 percent in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a projected minimum of 11.2 percent. Source:
federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-june-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-introduction.htm.

## How to use these anchors
To build a Fed-style severe scenario with the planning tools, impose a deep GDP decline over the first quarters
and an equity change of minus 58 percent over three quarters, then compare the model-implied VIX and rates with
the published values. Differences are expected: the expansion model is linear and Gaussian.
